Your data. Your device.

W Money privacy policy · Effective November 11, 2026 · Local release 1.1

W Money is a personal budgeting app published under the W Money name. This policy covers the local-only iPhone and iPad release.

Data stays in the app

Transactions, notes, categories, budgets, savings goals and currency settings are stored in the app’s files on your device. W Money does not transmit this ledger to the developer or a W Money server. No W Money account or bank connection is required or provided in this release.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. The developer does not collect identifiers, location or usage analytics through the app. Apple may handle device backups or diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policies.

On-device explanations

On supported devices, optional AI explanations use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. The app provides aggregate planner figures to the model, excluding raw merchant names, transaction notes, account identifiers and goal names. W Money does not send this information to a remote AI service. Generated explanations can be inaccurate; check them against the calculated plan.

Imports, exports and backups

You choose files to import and where to save or share CSV exports. Exported files can contain financial information. Keep them private and share them only with people or services you trust. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Your controls

You can edit or delete transactions and goals, export transactions, or erase the local ledger in Settings. Data remains until you remove it or delete the app, subject to device backups and any copies you export. The sample ledger is separate from your own records.

Support and privacy requests

Contact: wmoneyw@proton.me. If you contact support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond and resolve the request. Please do not send bank passwords, account numbers or complete transaction histories. W Money’s developer cannot access or remotely erase the ledger on your device.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The website uses no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process connection information to operate and secure its hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If W Money’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. Online banking and public rankings are not included in this release.
