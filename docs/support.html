A little help.

Questions about W Money? Contact wmoneyw@proton.me. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Never send bank passwords, account numbers or a full transaction history.

Start with a sample

On Overview, choose Explore a sample. The temporary sample ledger stays separate from your records. Tap Done to return to your own ledger.

Add spending and goals

Use the plus button in Activity to add a transaction. In Goals, create a target, record the amount already saved and optionally choose a target date. Tap a goal to edit it.

Import or export a statement

Open Settings and choose Import statement or Export transactions. CSV files without bank IDs can create duplicate entries if imported repeatedly. Export copies do not include goals.

Understand your plan

Overview → A plan for your money uses the last three complete calendar months. Missing months count as zero and the current month is excluded. Own-account transfers are excluded. These are estimates based on your records, not your bank balance. No money moves automatically.

AI availability

The calculated planner works without AI. Optional explanations require a supported Apple Intelligence device, iOS 26 or later, and an available on-device model.

Currency and privacy

Choose the ledger currency before adding records or goals. It stays fixed while the ledger contains activity, budgets or goals, because W Money does not convert currencies. To remove local information, use Settings → Erase this local ledger. Export transactions first if needed. See the privacy policy for details.

Bank connections

This local release does not connect bank accounts or provide worldwide rankings. Transactions can be entered manually or imported using CSV.
