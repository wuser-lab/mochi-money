# W Money

Subtitle: Spending, goals & clarity
Category: Finance
Version: 1.1.0
Price proposal: Free, no in-app purchases

## Description

A clearer view of your money. A little more room for what matters.

W Money brings your spending, income and savings goals into a calm, native iPhone experience. See your monthly cash flow, find where your money went and build a practical monthly rhythm for the things you want to save for.

YOUR MONEY, AT A GLANCE
See recorded income and spending in a focused overview, with category charts and recent activity.

GOALS WITH A PLAN
Set a target, track your progress and explore monthly contributions based on your recorded cash flow. Adjust the cushion you want to keep before allocating money to goals.

A LEDGER THAT TRAVELS WITH YOU
Add and edit transactions, search your activity, and import or export CSV statements. No account is required for local tracking.

OPTIONAL ON-DEVICE EXPLANATIONS
On supported Apple Intelligence devices, ask for an explanation of your calculated plan. Your raw transaction notes and account details aren't included. The planner works without AI.

DESIGNED TO FEEL AT HOME
Native navigation, readable charts, support for light and dark mode, and an isolated sample ledger to explore before adding your own information.

W Money is a budgeting tool. Its historical estimates and AI explanations are suggestions, not guarantees, investment advice or instructions to move money.

## Review notes

This release provides local budgeting only. No sign-in, bank connection, purchase or subscription is required. There are no live worldwide rankings.

Overview → Explore a sample opens a separate temporary sample ledger. Tap Done to return to the user's ledger. Sample data never merges with personal records.

Activity supports manual transactions; Settings supports CSV import/export. Goals supports creating and editing a target, amount already saved and optional date. Currency is fixed while the ledger contains records or goals to avoid relabeling existing amounts.

Overview → A plan for your money calculates suggestions from the last three complete months. Optional explanations use Apple's on-device Foundation Models on supported devices with Apple Intelligence. The calculated planner works without the model. No remote AI API is configured and no money moves automatically.

Privacy declaration for this build: data is stored on device and not collected by the developer through the app. Website hosting and voluntary support communications are described separately in the public policy.
